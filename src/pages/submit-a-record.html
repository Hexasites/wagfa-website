Five steps

From the deck to the record book

  1. Weigh & witness

    Weigh your fish in front of witnesses who can confirm you followed the GFAA Angling Rules. Unwitnessed catches may be disallowed if questions arise.

  2. Check the record

    Search the current State records and Australian records. Your fish must beat the existing record, or meet the minimum for a vacant one.

  3. Complete the claim

    Fill in every field on the claim form, include the required photographs and confirm your tackle meets the equipment rules.

  4. Pay the fee

    $20 per State claim, paid to WAGFA, and $20 per Australian claim, paid to GFAA. Attach the payment receipt to your claim.

  5. Lodge through your club

    Give the claim to your club weigh master and/or club secretary, who forward it to the WAGFA State Recorder.

Know the rules

Rules every claim must meet

A summary of key GFAA record requirements. The full GFAA Angling Rules always apply.

{{@icon users card__icon}}

Who can claim

Records can only be claimed by a financial or life member of a club affiliated with a State branch of GFAA.

{{@icon gf-lure card__icon}}

Sanctioned events only

GFAA does not accept record applications from within a non-sanctioned tournament, other than an internal club competition.

{{@icon gf-scale card__icon}}

Beating a record

Under 11 kg, a new record must be at least 50 g heavier. From 11 kg, at least 0.5% heavier (250 g at 50 kg). Anything less is a tie.

{{@icon gf-measure card__icon}}

Vacant records

A game fish must weigh at least the line class to claim a vacant record. Sharks and rays must weigh at least twice the line class.

{{@icon gf-rod card__icon}}

Juniors & Small Fry

Juniors have not turned 16 and Small Fry have not turned 11. Age is verified from the date of birth on the claim's statutory declaration.

{{@icon gf-tuna card__icon}}

Identification

If the species could be in doubt from photos, keep the fish preserved or frozen until a qualified authority can verify it.

{{@icon triangle-alert}}

No claim will be accepted for fish caught in hatchery waters, sanctuaries or banned fishing areas, or caught contrary to the laws governing the species or the waters.
