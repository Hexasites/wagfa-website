For clubs

Sanctioning a tournament

Sanctioning confirms your tournament meets WAGFA's requirements, and it protects your anglers' record claims.

The Game Fishing Association of Australia does not accept record applications generated from within a non-sanctioned tournament, other than an internal club competition.

  1. Download the pack

    Read the overview and requirements in the Tournament Sanctioning Pack.

  2. Request sanctioning

    Complete the request form and send it to the WAGFA Secretary ahead of your event.

  3. Report afterwards

    After the event, complete and return the tournament report form.
