Course outline

Seven sections, one goal

The course is split into seven sections. Three are recommended for every angler; all seven are required for anyone who wants to become a certified weigh master.

Each section ends with a self-evaluation quiz, which you complete before moving on.

  1. 01

    Equipment rules

    Line, leaders, hooks, rods, reels, gaffs and the other equipment regulations that apply to every capture.

  2. 02

    Angling rules

    The rules that govern how a fish is hooked, fought and landed.

  3. 03

    All-tackle length records

    How measure-and-release length records work.

  4. 04

    Fly fishing

    The rules and equipment for fly records.

  5. 05

    Eligible species

    Which species are eligible for records.

  6. 06

    Record applications

    Preparing, checking and lodging State and Australian record claims.

  7. 07

    Assessment

    The final self-assessment.

Accreditation

Accreditation as a weigh master is by a separate evaluation administered by WAGFA.

Ask about the course

Why it matters

Weigh masters are the first check on every claim. All record claims are submitted through the club weigh master and/or club secretary to the State Recorder.
