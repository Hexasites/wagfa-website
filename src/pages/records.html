How records work

Categories & classes

Angler categories
Male, Female, Junior Male, Junior Female, Small Fry Male and Small Fry Female. Juniors have not turned 16; Small Fry have not turned 11.
Line classes
1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 24, 37 and 60 kg, based on the wet-tested breaking strength of the line used.
Environments
Saltwater, Saltwater Land-Based, Saltwater Fly, Freshwater and Freshwater Fly.
Vacant records
A game fish must weigh at least the line class to claim a vacant record; sharks and rays at least twice the line class.
