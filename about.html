Who we are

Coordinating game fishing across Western Australia

The Western Australian Game Fishing Association was established in 1949. It coordinates the activities of game fishing throughout Western Australia on behalf of its member clubs.

WAGFA maintains the State game fishing records, holds data on the open tournaments run by its member clubs, and plays a major role in advocacy on behalf of those clubs and game fishing in general.

The Association was a founding member of Recfishwest, the State's peak recreational fishing body, and has been represented at board or reference-group level since Recfishwest began. Nationally, WAGFA is a member of the Game Fishing Association of Australia (GFAA), and internationally of the International Game Fish Association (IGFA).

The Objects of the Association focus strongly on conservation and on promoting ethical game fishing practices.

What we do

Four things we do for WA game fishing

Keep the records

Our State Recorder maintains the official Western Australian game fishing records for men, women, juniors and small fry across every line class.

Sanction tournaments

We sanction member-club tournaments and hold data on the open tournaments run across the State.

Advocate

We speak up for our member clubs and for game fishing, including through Recfishwest and the Game Fishing Association of Australia.

Promote ethical fishing

Conservation and ethical practice sit at the heart of our Objects, from tag and release to the rules that govern every capture.

Our history

More than 77 years of game fishing

WAGFA's story begins in 1949, with a group of anglers whose boats could venture to Rottnest Island and beyond.

Today the Association represents 12 clubs, from the tropical waters of the Kimberley to Geographe Bay in the South West.

  1. 1938

    A national body is formed

    The national game fishing body, now the Game Fishing Association of Australia, is founded so anglers across the country have a voice and representation.

  2. 1949

    Game fishing organises in WA

    Around 20 anglers with boats that could venture to Rottnest Island and beyond form a club originally known as the Western Australian Game Fishing Association. Spanish mackerel were plentiful, and marlin were the pinnacle species.

  3. Later

    A peak body for the State

    The original club changes its name to the Perth Game Fishing Club, so the WAGFA name can be used by the peak State body representing game fishing.

  4. Recfishwest

    A founding member

    WAGFA becomes a founding member of Recfishwest, the State's peak recreational fishing body, and is represented at board or reference-group level from its beginning.

  5. Today

    12 clubs, 900 records

    Member clubs from Broome to Busselton, and 900 current State records across 53 species.
